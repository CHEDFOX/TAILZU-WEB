The team

  • A voice keyboard that turns what you say into clean, ready-to-send text, wherever you type. Filler goes, punctuation comes in, and names stay the way you said them. It writes a little differently for each app: easy in chats, clear and polite in mail, short at work.

  • Your own calendar and to-do list, inside the app. It shows how long it takes to get from one place to the next, tells you when to leave, and reminds you before it matters.

    gym tomorrow at 7On your day. Tomorrow, 7:00 am. 12 minutes from home.

  • One place for a trip: the dates, the tickets, the weather on your days, stays compared by how far they are from where you will be, and a packing list. It finds the options and opens them for you to book. You always press pay.

  • Tell it what to build or fix. It reads your project, works on a safe copy, tests every change and asks when it needs you. Nothing goes live until you say yes.

  • Make a space for home, for friends or for a team. Share lists, trips, plans and files, chat one-to-one or in groups, and give someone a task that lands on their day. You choose what each person can do.

    share the packing list with ammaShared. She can add to it, and nothing else.

  • X0 Bot drafts it with a name, its rules and what it may use. Try it, change it, and it joins your team. You can also add bots other people have made.

On your phone. On your computer.

Sign in on your computer by scanning a code with your phone, and your bots, notes and chats are on both. The keyboard is in every app. The widget and the Lock Screen show what your bots are working on.
